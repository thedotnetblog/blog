## Weishenme zhe dui .NET tuandui zhongyao

Zhe lei gengxin tongchang neng zai jiaofu sudu pingtai yizhixing he zhili zhijian tigong geng hao pingheng.

## Shicao jianyi

1. Xian zai xiaoguimo .NET shidian zhong yanzheng shiyong jiejin shengchan de shuju.
2. Kuozhan qian xian dingyi keguancexing yu huigun jianchadian.
3. Ba luodi zuofa xieru neibu muban fangbian qita tuandui fuyong.
